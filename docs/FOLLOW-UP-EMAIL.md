# Second email (if Anya agrees to go ahead)

The first email pitches the website concept. If Anya says yes, send a short second email asking for what the site still needs. The most valuable ask goes first.

## 1. Instagram performance report (the big one)

Ask Anya for a recent **Instagram Insights report** (or a screenshot export) so the Partnerships page can show her real numbers instead of third-party estimates. It will be a strong selling point for sponsored posts and collaborations.

What to ask for, in plain terms:
- **Followers** and follower growth over the last 90 days.
- **Reach** and **impressions** (accounts reached, unique vs total) for the last 30 and 90 days.
- **Reels views and plays** for recent posts, plus her **average** views per Reel.
- **Engagement**: likes, comments, saves, shares, and engagement rate.
- **Audience demographics**: age, gender, top cities and countries.
- **Results from sponsored or partner posts**, if she is happy to share them (clicks, link taps, saves, codes used).

How it will be used: each figure goes into `src/data/metrics.json` with its source, date range and a note that it is Anya's own Instagram Insights, replacing the provisional Fohr numbers.

## 2. Confirmations the site needs

- Which **TV shows are current**, and any she wants added or removed.
- **Bio claims** to confirm: ShoeDazzle and YouCeleb titles, the POSHGLAM award, FIDM and USC teaching, writing for Us Weekly and OK!, Extra.
- Whether she is happy to **publish** her email address, and her **current official logos and marks** (and an **Extra** logo).
- **Photo and video rights** for the KTLA Morning News and TEDx photographs and the KTLA clips, with credit lines, plus **captions or transcripts** for the video.
- Approval of the **AI-assisted imagery** and the wording of the "How the work feels" pillars.
